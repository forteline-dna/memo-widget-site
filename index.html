Memo Widget

Today's schedule, to-dos and notes — always in view.

Memo Widget is a small widget that stays on your desktop (macOS, Windows) or phone (Android, iPhone) and shows the things you need today in one place:

  • Today's events from your Google Calendar — view, add, edit and delete events.
  • To-dos from Google Tasks — add tasks, check them off, manage task lists.
  • Notes — long-form memos that sync between your devices.
  • Settings sync — your widget settings follow you to every device.

How your Google data is used

Memo Widget asks you to sign in with Google only so it can show and edit your own calendar events and tasks, and keep your notes and settings in a private app folder in your own Google Drive. The app talks directly to Google from your device. There is no Memo Widget server, and the developer never receives your data. Read the full Privacy Policy.
